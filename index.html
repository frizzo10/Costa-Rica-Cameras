How We Build It

The rules that don't change, no matter what feature we're on

Not a mission statement. These are the actual constraints that have shaped every real decision in this app so far — meant to keep shaping the next one too.

01

Real data, or an honest gap — never a third option

No invented baseline, no fabricated completion, no guessed number dressed up as a measurement. If the ring hasn't measured it, or the person hasn't said it, the product says so out loud instead of filling the space with something plausible-looking.

02

Coaching, not medicine

Every specialist is a coach, not a clinician. None of them diagnose. Dr. Sage's whole job is knowing when something's worth a real doctor's attention and saying so plainly — never pretending the team is a substitute for one.

03

Difficulty scales to the real person

A stated beginner never gets an athlete's plan with an easier-sounding label slapped on it. Regimens, mindfulness plans, and meal plans all adapt to real readiness data and real week-to-week completion — harder when it's earned, easier when the data says to ease off.

04

Commitment is the person's own words

Goals, weekly promises, and plan check-ins get reflected back in what the person actually said — voice or typed, saved verbatim — and confirmed by them before anything counts as decided. Nothing about someone's stated goal changes silently, even when the data suggests it should.

05

A safety net that doesn't depend on the model's mood

Every real conversation surface runs a deterministic crisis-language check independent of whatever the AI itself would say — same real resources, every time, regardless of how the model is behaving that day.

06

Every plan points at one real goal

Four specialists, four domains — but the goal a person actually stated is what every one of their plans has to serve. Not generic domain advice five different ways.

myDrSage — built one honest constraint at a time.
